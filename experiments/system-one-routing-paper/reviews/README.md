# Construction and review provenance

The files here retain AI author/first-review snapshots and anonymous human summary
records. Final pre-call inputs and labels live in `../datasets/`. Historical notes
may describe a then-pending review or expert prerequisite; later decisions are
explained in the manuscript and data card, not retroactively edited into the notes.

Engineering's original author and coverage-supplement author are sequential AI
sources. The original cohort has its own anonymous-first-label/reconciliation
process. The review brief describes the procedural access restrictions. The
corresponding `author-*` and `reviewer-initial-*` JSONL snapshots preserve both
positions; matched comparisons reuse the final unchanged conversations.

Mathematical files include the author notes, AI-reviewed labels before human
adjudication, and the pre-call component change ledger. MR010_b retains its original
insufficient action label; the separate sensitivity is in `../sensitivities/`.

The human summaries cover two AI-assisted people each reviewing 96 historical
engineering records, and two AI-assisted people each reviewing 64 mathematical
records. Mathematics had no domain-expert reviewer. Raw submissions, identity
mappings, invitation links and personal assistant conversations are excluded.

The disclosed workflow counts are resampling units, not independent human authors
or model architectures. Author/reviewer model identities are undisclosed. Anonymous
AI review does not establish independent human or domain-expert validation.
