# HiRoute review and integration

Read for authorized review or publication. Inspect the complete candidate against the selected Issue/Design, effective answers, durable specs, code and existing evidence. Review product direction, responsibility boundaries, normal/failure paths and observable acceptance; do not infer success from source inspection alone.

Ordinary changes proceed to human PR review. If the user explicitly selected a coordinated stage, its designated coordinator performs stage alignment and convergence in its own capable session. Shared review instructions do not require Claude to load any Codex-only skill. Self-inspection and self-tests are useful but must not be described as independent review.

Record actionable findings and handoff in the corresponding Issue/PR or the requested provider comments. Name the reviewed revision, concrete failure or violated invariant, expected correction and focused verification. Return repairs to their current owner. Do not add typed REVIEW/VERIFY, readiness receipts, rationale quotas or another tracking system.

Use [HiRoute validation](../../.agents/skills/hiroute-validation/SKILL.md) to interpret evidence and select any necessary checks, `hiroute-integrate` for authorized branch convergence, and `hiroute-public-pr` before public pushes. Review alone grants no publication or merge authority. Follow GitHub's review/check rules; the human decides approval and merge.
